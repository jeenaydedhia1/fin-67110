# COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP /NEW (COST) — Equity Research Report

## 1. Overview
#### Business model

- **Format.** "We operate membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept that offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products in a wide range of categories will produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover." [10-K Item 1]
- **Low-margin, high-efficiency operation.** "…these volumes and turnover enable us to operate profitably at significantly lower gross margins (net sales less merchandise costs) than most other retailers." [10-K Item 1] The company also says: "We often sell inventory before we are required to pay for it, even while taking advantage of early payment discounts." [10-K Item 1]
- **Limited assortment.** "We carry less than 4,000 active stock keeping units (SKUs) per warehouse in our core warehouse business, significantly less than other broadline retailers." [10-K Item 1]
- **Distribution.** "Our depots receive large shipments from suppliers and quickly ship these goods to warehouses." [10-K Item 1]
- **Footprint.** "Costco operated 939, 914, and 890 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026, August 31, 2025, and September 1, 2024." [10-K Item 1] The release lists "647 in the United States and Puerto Rico, 115 in Canada, 43 in Mexico, 37 in Japan, 29 in the United Kingdom, 20 in Korea, 15 in Australia, 14 in Taiwan, seven in China, five in Spain, three in France, two in Sweden, and one each in Iceland, and New Zealand." [Release]
- **Revenue mix.**
  - "Our gasoline business represented 11% of total net sales in 2026." [10-K Item 1]
  - "Digitally-enabled sales, which represents sales that are initiated through a digital device, whether fulfilled through a warehouse or distribution center, as well as Costco Travel, represented 11% of total net sales in 2026." [10-K Item 1]
- **Membership as the profit anchor.**
  - "The membership format is integral to our business and profitability." [10-K Item 7]
  - "Our annual fee for memberships is $65 in the U.S., varies in other countries and includes a free household card." [10-K Item 1]
  - "Executive members represented 42,300, 38,700, and 35,400 of total paid members in 2026, 2025, and 2024." [10-K Item 1]
  - "The penetration of sales to Executive members represented approximately 75.2% of worldwide net sales in 2026." [10-K Item 1]
  - "Our member renewal rate was 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada and 89.8% worldwide at the end of 2026." [10-K Item 1]
- **Profit drivers named by management.**
  - "We believe that the most important driver of our profitability is increasing net sales, particularly comparable sales." [10-K Item 7]
  - "Because our business operates on very low margins, modest changes in various items in the consolidated statements of income, particularly merchandise costs and SG&A expenses, can have substantial impacts on net income." [10-K Item 7]

## 2. Market Positioning and Growth Outlook
#### Competitive position

- **Competitors.** "We compete on a worldwide basis with global, national, and regional wholesalers and retailers, including supermarkets, supercenters, online retailers, gasoline stations, hard discounters, department and specialty stores, and operators selling a single category or narrow range of merchandise." [10-K Item 1]
  - "Walmart, Target, Kroger, and Amazon are among our significant general merchandise retail competitors in the U.S." [10-K Item 1]
  - "We also compete with other warehouse clubs, including Walmart's Sam's Club and BJ's Wholesale Club in the U.S." [10-K Item 1]
  - "Many of the metropolitan areas in the U.S. and certain of our Other International locations have multiple competing clubs." [10-K Item 1]
- **Pricing authority.** "We do not focus in the short-term on maximizing prices charged, but instead seek to maintain what we believe is a perception among our members of our 'pricing authority' – consistently providing the most competitive values." [10-K Item 7]
- **Private label.** "We believe that Kirkland Signature products are high quality, offered at prices that are generally lower than national brands, and help lower costs, differentiate our merchandise offerings, and generally earn higher margins. We expect to continue to increase the sales penetration of our private-label items." [10-K Item 1]
- **Supplier base.** "We do not obtain a significant portion of merchandise from any one supplier." [10-K Item 1]
- **Shrink and labor efficiency.** "By strictly controlling the entrances and exits and using a membership format, we believe our inventory losses (shrinkage) are well below those of typical retail operations." [10-K Item 1]
- **Workforce.** "In March 2026, we increased the top of wage scales by $1.00 per hour in the U.S. and Canada, bringing our average rate at the end of 2026 for hourly employees in the U.S. to approximately $33.00." [10-K Item 1] "In 2026, in the U.S. and Canada that rate was approximately 94% for employees who have been with us for at least one year." [10-K Item 1]

#### Growth drivers evidenced in fiscal 2026

- **Comparable sales.**
  - "Comparable sales were positively impacted by increases of 5% in average ticket and 3% in shopping frequency." [10-K Item 7]
  - The 10-K table shows "Total Company 8 %" for comparable sales and "Total Company 7 %" excluding foreign-currency and gasoline. [10-K Item 7]
  - The release reports "Total Company 9.4% 6.7% 8.4% 6.6%". [Release]
- **Digital.** "Digitally-enabled comparable sales increased 21% during 2026, both as reported and excluding the impact of changes in foreign currencies." [10-K Item 7]
- **Mix.** "Sales increased $17,136 or 8% in core merchandise categories, increasing in all categories. Sales in warehouse ancillary and other businesses increased $10,199, or 20%, led by gasoline and pharmacy." [10-K Item 7]
- **Gasoline.** "The volume of gasoline sold increased 7%, positively impacting net sales by $1,913, or 71 basis points." [10-K Item 7]
- **Membership.**
  - The membership table shows "Total cardholders 150,400 145,200 136,800" for 2026, 2025 and 2024. [10-K Item 1]
  - "As previously reported, we increased annual membership fees in the U.S. and Canada, effective September 1, 2024." [10-K Item 7]
  - "The fee income increase accounted for approximately 30% and 40% of membership income growth during 2026 and 2025." [10-K Item 7]
- **New warehouses.** "We opened 28 new warehouses, including three relocations, for a total of 25 net new warehouses: 18 in the U.S., five in our Canadian segment, and two in our Other International segment…" [10-K Item 7]
- **International runway.** "Our rate of square footage growth is generally higher in many of our international markets, due to the smaller base in those markets, and we expect that to continue." [10-K Item 7]

#### Margin and cost considerations

- **Gross margin mix.** "This increase was positively impacted by 19 basis points in warehouse ancillary and other businesses, primarily gasoline and pharmacy, offset by an 11 basis point decrease in core merchandise categories." [10-K Item 7]
- **Core margin.** "The gross margin in core merchandise categories when expressed as a percentage of their own sales increased 15 basis points." [10-K Item 7]
- **Co-brand card cost.** "Core merchandise categories were also negatively impacted by our co-branded credit card program as a result of reward costs associated with higher gasoline sales." [10-K Item 7]
- **Gasoline sensitivity.** "A higher penetration of gasoline sales will generally lower our gross margin percentage." [10-K Item 7]
- **Pricing philosophy.** "Our investments in merchandise pricing may include reducing prices on merchandise to drive sales or meet competition and holding prices steady despite cost increases instead of passing the increases on to our members, negatively impacting gross margin and gross margin as a percentage of net sales (gross margin percentage) in the near term." [10-K Item 7]
- **Cost pressures outside management's control.** "…some significant costs are partially outside our control, particularly health care and utility expenses." [10-K Item 7]
- **Tariffs.** "Higher tariffs are more likely to adversely impact rather than improve our results." [10-K Item 7]
- **New-warehouse drag.** "Negative aspects of such growth include lower initial operating profitability relative to existing warehouses and cannibalization of sales at existing warehouses when openings occur in existing markets." [10-K Item 7]
- **Renewal-rate drag.** "Our worldwide renewal rate is adversely impacted by membership growth in certain international markets and a higher penetration of memberships sold online, including through digital membership promotions, which renew at a slightly lower rate on average." [10-K Item 7]
- **Seasonality.** "The material seasonal impact in our operations is increased net sales and earnings during the winter holiday season." [10-K Item 1]

- **Growth.** FY2026 total revenue was $303,154M, up 10.14%. Operating income was $11,685M, up 12.54%. GAAP diluted EPS was $20.76, up 14.00% from $18.21. Earnings grew faster than revenue.
- **Membership model.** Membership fee revenue rose 11% to $5,907. Worldwide renewal was 89.8% and U.S./Canada renewal was 92.3%. Executive members accounted for approximately 75.2% of worldwide net sales.
- **Underlying sales.** Total company comparable sales rose 8.4% for the year. Excluding gasoline prices and foreign exchange they rose 6.6%. Digitally-enabled comparable sales rose 20.9%.
- **Balance sheet.** Return on average equity is 28.40%. Debt to equity is 0.17x. Free cash flow was $9,382 million.
- **Runway.** Management plans up to 33 new warehouses in 2027, and capex is intended to rise to approximately $7,500.

#### Why not a Buy
- **Valuation.** The stock trades at 47.8x trailing GAAP EPS. The free cash flow yield is about 2.1% (2.13%) on a market cap of about $439.8B. The Financial Analyst's view is that this price pays for durable high-single to low-double-digit growth and leaves little room for a slowdown.
- **No margin edge.** Operating margin is 3.85%. That is in line with BJ's at 3.81% and below Walmart at 4.18% and Target at 4.88%. The peer fiscal years end 2026-01-31, so the comparison is approximate. The premium rests on membership stability and comparable-sales growth, not on superior margins.
- **Some Q4 strength is one-off.** Q4 GAAP EPS was $6.75 against $5.87. It included a non-recurring benefit of $0.15 per diluted share from IEEPA tariff refunds. The refunds received were small ($184 in 2026), and management intends to reinvest the majority of them in member value.
- **Margin headwinds.** Gross margin percentage decreased three basis points. Higher gasoline penetration lowers gross margin percentage. Digital sales carry some lower gross-margin percentage. Health care and wage costs are partly outside management's control. The top of the wage scale rose by $1.00 per hour.
- **No guidance.** The filings give no quantitative FY2027 sales or earnings guidance. There are no forward estimates, so the Financial Analyst made no price target.

#### Why not a Sell
Revenue, earnings and cash flow are all growing at double-digit rates. Membership economics are strong, leverage is low, and there is a long runway of new warehouses, especially international ones. Nothing in the filings shows deterioration. The risk is the multiple, not the business.

#### The deciding number
**47.8x** trailing GAAP EPS. If growth holds near the FY2026 pace, the premium is defensible. If comparable sales slow or margins slip, there is little cushion. That balance supports Hold.

#### Data caveat
The 10-K shows operating cash flow of $15,817 and the release shows 15,825. The release statements are labelled "Subject to Reclassification". The Financial Analyst used the 10-K figure and says the difference does not change the conclusion.
