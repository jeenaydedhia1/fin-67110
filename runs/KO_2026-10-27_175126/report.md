# COCA COLA CO (KO) — Equity Research Report

## 1. Overview
#### Overview

**Source note:** The 10-K covers fiscal 2025. The 8-K Exhibit 99.1 is the third-quarter 2026 release dated Oct. 27, 2026. Each fact is labeled with its source.

#### Business model
- **Scope.** "The Coca-Cola Company is a total beverage company, and beverage products bearing our trademarks, sold in the United States since 1886, are now sold in more than 200 countries and territories." [10-K Item 1]
- **Scale of consumption.** "Beverages bearing trademarks owned by or licensed to the Company account for 2.2 billion of the estimated 65 billion servings of all beverages consumed worldwide every day." [10-K Item 1]
- **Two lines of business.** "Our Company operates in two lines of business: concentrate operations and finished product operations." [10-K Item 7]
  - Concentrate: "Our concentrate operations typically generate net operating revenues by selling beverage concentrates, sometimes referred to as “beverage bases,” syrups, including fountain syrups, and certain finished beverages to authorized bottling operations". [10-K Item 1]
  - Finished product: "Generally, finished product operations generate higher net operating revenues but lower gross profit margins than concentrate operations." [10-K Item 1]
  - Revenue split: "Concentrate operations 59 % 59 %" and "Finished product operations 41 41" for 2025 and 2024. [10-K Item 7]
  - Volume split: "Concentrate operations 85 % 85 %". [10-K Item 7]
- **Bottler contracts.**
  - "The bottler is obligated to purchase its entire requirement of concentrates or syrups for the designated Company Trademark Beverages from the Company or Company-authorized suppliers." [10-K Item 1]
  - "we have implemented an incidence-based concentrate pricing model in most markets." [10-K Item 1]
  - "the concentrate price we charge is impacted by a number of factors, including, but not limited to, bottler pricing, the channels in which the finished products produced from the concentrates are sold, and package mix." [10-K Item 1]
- **Bottling ownership is selective.**
  - "from time to time we acquire or take control of a bottling operation, often in underperforming markets where we believe we can use our resources and expertise to improve performance." [10-K Item 1]
  - "we may periodically consider options for divesting or reducing our ownership interest in a consolidated bottling operation". [10-K Item 1]
- **Segments.** "Our operating structure includes the following operating segments:" EMEA, Latin America, North America, Asia Pacific and Bottling Investments. [10-K Item 1]
- **Structure change.** "Effective January 1, 2025, we sunset our Global Ventures operating segment to streamline and simplify our operating structure." [10-K Item 1]
- **Alcohol.** "The Company has also directly entered the alcohol beverage category in numerous markets outside the United States." [10-K Item 1]
- **Brands.** "sparkling soft drinks: Coca-Cola, Diet Coke/Coca-Cola Light, Coca-Cola Zero Sugar, Fanta, Fresca, Schweppes". Other categories include Costa, fairlife and BODYARMOR. [10-K Item 1]

#### Volume and mix (FY2025)
- "The Coca-Cola system sold 33.8 billion and 33.7 billion unit cases of our products in 2025 and 2024, respectively." [10-K Item 1]
- "Sparkling soft drinks represented 69% of our worldwide unit case volume in both 2025 and 2024." [10-K Item 1]
- "Trademark Coca-Cola accounted for 47% of our worldwide unit case volume in both 2025 and 2024." [10-K Item 1]
- "Unit case volume outside the United States represented 84% of the Company’s worldwide unit case volume in 2025." [10-K Item 1]
- "In 2025, these five bottling partners combined represented 44% of our total worldwide unit case volume." [10-K Item 1]

#### FY2025 financial performance
- "Net operating revenues were $47,941 million in 2025, compared to $47,061 million in 2024, an increase of $880 million, or 2%." [10-K Item 7]
- "Price/mix had a 4% favorable impact on our consolidated net operating revenues." [10-K Item 7]
- "Fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates, including the effects of our hedging activities, unfavorably impacted our consolidated net operating revenues by 2%." [10-K Item 7]
- "Our gross profit margin increased to 61.6% in 2025 from 61.1% in 2024." [10-K Item 7]
- "Advertising expenses for 2025 and 2024 were $5.4 billion and $5.1 billion, respectively." [10-K Item 7]

#### Latest quarter (Q3 2026)

## 2. Market Positioning and Growth Outlook
#### Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

#### Competitive position
- **Competitors.** "In many of the countries in which we do business, PepsiCo, Inc. is a primary competitor." Others named: "Nestlé S.A., Keurig Dr Pepper Inc., Danone S.A., Suntory Beverage & Food Limited, Anheuser-Busch InBev, Kirin Holdings, Heineken N.V., Diageo plc and Red Bull GmbH." [10-K Item 1]
- **Stated strengths.** "Our competitive strengths include leading brands with high levels of consumer recognition and loyalty; a worldwide network of bottlers and distributors of Company products; sophisticated marketing capabilities; and a talented group of dedicated employees." [10-K Item 1]
- **Stated challenges.** "Our competitive challenges include strong competitors in all geographic regions; in many countries, a concentrated retail sector with powerful buyers able to freely choose among Company products, products of competitive beverage suppliers and individual retailers’ own store or private-label beverage brands; new industry entrants; and dramatic shifts in consumer shopping methods and patterns due to a rapidly evolving digital landscape." [10-K Item 1]
- **Formula protection.** "Trade secrets are an important aspect of our technology, and our sparkling beverage and other beverage formulas are among the important trade secrets of our Company." [10-K Item 1]
- **Value share, Q3 2026.**
  - Total: "The company gained value share in total nonalcoholic ready-to-drink (“NARTD”) beverages." [Release]
  - EMEA: "The company lost value share in total NARTD beverages, as gains in Nigeria and Morocco were more than offset by losses in France and the United Kingdom." [Release]
  - Latin America: "The company gained value share in total NARTD beverages, led by share gains in Brazil and Mexico." [Release]
  - North America: "The company gained value share in total NARTD beverages, led by share gains in juice, value-added dairy and plant-based beverages and Trademark Coca-Cola." [Release]
  - Asia Pacific: "The company gained value share in total NARTD beverages, led by share gains in Japan and Vietnam." [Release]

#### Growth strategy
- "We are focused on the following growth pillars: shaping a portfolio of loved brands; transforming our marketing and innovation agenda; optimizing the Coca-Cola ecosystem; building talent and capabilities; and enhancing our license to win." [10-K Item 7]
- "we are increasing our investments in e-commerce to support retail and meal delivery services, offering more package sizes that are fit-for-purpose for online sales and shifting more consumer and trade promotions to digital." [10-K Item 7]

#### Growth trajectory
- **FY2025 (10-K): modest volume.**
  - "In 2025, worldwide concentrate sales volume grew 1% and unit case volume was even compared to 2024." [10-K Item 7]
  - "Unit case volume in North America decreased 1%". [10-K Item 7]
  - "The operating segment’s volume performance included 2% growth in Brazil and 6% growth in Argentina, offset by a decline of 4% in Mexico." [10-K Item 7]
  - "Unit case volume in EMEA increased 3%". [10-K Item 7]
- **Q3 2026 (release): faster volume.**
  - "Unit case volume grew 5%, led by India, China, Mexico and Brazil." [Release]
  - "Trademark Coca-Cola grew 5% and Coca-Cola Zero Sugar grew 16%". [Release]
  - "Water grew 8%". [Release]
  - "Concentrate sales were 2 points ahead of unit case volume due to the timing of concentrate shipments." [Release]
  - North America: "Unit case volume was even, as growth in Trademark Coca-Cola was offset by a decline in water, sports, coffee and tea." [Release]
  - Asia Pacific: "Price/mix declined 2%, as pricing actions in the marketplace were more than offset by unfavorable mix." [Release]
- **Nine months 2026.** Net operating revenues are shown as "Net Operating Revenues $ 39,232 $ 36,119 9", and operating income as "Operating Income 13,496 11,921 13". [Release]

#### Weak spots flagged in the filings
- **BODYARMOR.** "the Company concluded that the fair value of the trademark was less than its carrying value and recorded an additional impairment charge of $960 million." [10-K Item 7]
  - Cause: "a slowing of the projected long-term growth rate for the category, an intensifying competitive environment, and more focused innovation and international rollout plans." [10-K Item 7]
  - "The remaining carrying value of the trademark is $2,440 million." [10-K Item 7]
  - "it is likely that we would be required to recognize an additional impairment charge." [10-K Item 7]
  - Q3 2026: "Sports drinks declined 3%". [Release]
- **Health concerns.** "There is ongoing concern among consumers, public health professionals and governments about the health problems associated with obesity and other chronic diseases, which may present a challenge to our industry." [10-K Item 7]
- **Regulation.** "Various jurisdictions have adopted, and others may seek to adopt, bans or restrictions on the use of certain ingredients or substances in products". [10-K Item 1]
- **Input risk.** "Adverse weather conditions may affect the supply of agricultural commodities from which key ingredients for our products are derived and manufactured." [10-K Item 1]

#### Summary

**Valuation at $91.18 (2026-10-27).** The P/E on FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS of $3.04 is **30.0x** (91.18 / 3.04, computed in code). The EPS is from 10-K 0001628280-26-010047, tag EarningsPerShareDiluted, period end 2025-12-31.

**Trailing-twelve-month cross-check (my derivation, approximate).**
- I build TTM GAAP EPS as FY2025 EPS, less nine-month 2025 EPS, plus nine-month 2026 EPS.
- The EPS inputs are $3.04 from the 10-K, plus $2.86 and $2.51 from the Q3 release (8-K 0001628280-26-067403).
- Calculator: 3.04 - 2.51 + 2.86 = **$3.39**.
- Calculator: 91.18 / 3.39 = **26.9x**.
- Quarterly EPS figures are not strictly additive because share counts differ slightly, so treat this as approximate.
- The two multiples are both GAAP. I use no adjusted EPS.

I could not compute peer P/E ratios. No peer prices or EPS were supplied.

#### Growth

- FY2025 total revenue was $47,941m, up **1.87%** (47,941 / 47,061 - 1). Top-line growth was modest for the year.
- Q3 2026 net operating revenues were $13,380m against $12,455m, up **7.43%** (calculator). The release rounds this to 7%.

#### View

At 30.0x FY2025 GAAP EPS, or about 26.9x TTM, KO's valuation rests on the following:
1. A top-tier margin, 28.71% for FY2025 and a 33.4% GAAP margin in Q3 2026.
2. Accelerating growth. Q3 revenue was up 7% and EPS up 8%, and management raised its 2026 guidance. Comparable EPS growth is guided to at least 11% (non-GAAP, context only).
3. Declining leverage.

Risks:
- Divestitures will cut reported revenue by about 8% in 2027, with a roughly 2% EPS headwind.
- The ongoing IRS tax litigation is excluded from the underlying tax rate guidance.
- Currency tailwinds may fade (the release guides to a slight headwind in 2027).
- FY2025 free cash flow was weak against net income.

The context gives no price targets, peer multiples or dividend yield, so I make no relative-value call beyond the margin comparison above.

  - Downgrade to Sell if growth fades to the FY2025 pace of 1.87% as currency and calendar effects reverse, or if the IRS or BODYARMOR risks materialize at this valuation.

## 5. Investment Recommendation
**Rating: Hold**

**Deciding number:** 30.0x. This is the P/E at $91.18 (2026-10-27) on FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS of $3.04 (91.18 / 3.04), from the Financial Analyst's valuation narrative.

#### Rating: Hold

**Coca-Cola is a high-quality business, and the share price already reflects that. The one number that decides the rating is 30.0x.** At $91.18 on 2026-10-27, the shares trade at 30.0x FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS of $3.04. The Financial Analyst's approximate trailing-twelve-month cross-check is 26.9x on $3.39. Both multiples are GAAP. Neither is cheap for a business that guides to about 5% organic growth.

#### What supports the stock
- **Margins are top-tier.** FY2025 operating margin was 28.71%, against 21.23% in FY2024. That is close to Monster at 29.17% and well above Keurig Dr Pepper at 21.53% and PepsiCo at 12.24%. Q3 2026 GAAP operating margin was 33.4% versus 32.0% a year earlier.
- **Growth is accelerating.** FY2025 revenue growth was only 1.87%. Q3 2026 net revenues grew 7.43% to $13,380 million. Unit case volume grew 5%, and the company gained value share in total NARTD beverages.
- **Management raised guidance.** Comparable EPS growth is now guided to at least 11%, against 9% to 10% before. Free cash flow is guided to at least $13.0 billion, against approximately $12.4 billion before. Both are non-GAAP.
- **Leverage is falling.** Debt to equity was 1.37x at 2025-12-31. The Financial Analyst calculates 1.11x at 2026-10-02, though the two dates use different bases.
- **Returns are high.** Return on average equity was 45.97%. The Financial Analyst notes this is flattered by a modest equity base.

#### Why this is not a Buy at 30.0x
- **Some growth is not durable.** The Financial Analyst attributes part of the acceleration to currency tailwinds and six extra days in Q1 2026. Q4 2026 has six fewer days, and the 2027 guide shows a slight currency headwind.
- **Reported numbers will shrink.** If the Africa bottling sale closes, divestitures are a headwind of about 8% to 2027 revenue and about 2% to 2027 EPS. This is mostly a reporting effect, but it limits the headline growth that could justify a higher multiple.
- **The FY2025 margin is a GAAP figure.** The Financial Analyst could not itemize FY2024 charges, so the step-up from 21.23% to 28.71% may include comparability items. FY2025 free cash flow of $5,296 million was well below net income of $13,107 million.
- **Weak spots are visible.**
  - BODYARMOR carries $2,440 million of trademark value after a $960 million impairment. The company says another impairment is likely if results miss projections, and sports drinks declined 3% in Q3.
  - EMEA lost value share, with losses in France and the United Kingdom.
  - North America volume was even.
  - Asia Pacific price/mix declined 2%.
- **Tax litigation is not in the guide.** The tax-rate guide excludes the outcome of the IRS litigation if the company does not prevail.
- **No relative-value support.** There are no peer multiples or price target in the inputs, so valuation cannot be called cheap against peers.

**Conclusion:** Quality and momentum are real, but 30.0x leaves little room for the 2027 reporting headwinds or a margin slip. We would hold existing positions and wait for a better entry point or for evidence that earnings growth outruns the multiple.
